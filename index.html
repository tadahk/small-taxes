small taxes

two small inconveniences for your browser.

doomscroll tax

the longer you scroll a feed, the slower the page scrolls. close the tab and it resets.

tab tax

every tab past ten makes all your pages a little greyer, until you close some.

download

install

  1. unzip small-taxes.zip
  2. open chrome://extensions (works in chrome, brave, edge, arc, dia, vivaldi)
  3. turn on developer mode, top right
  4. click load unpacked and pick the small-taxes folder

the icon lets you turn either one off, change the tab limit, or edit which sites count as feeds. nothing leaves your browser. source · MIT
